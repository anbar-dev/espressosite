## Grinder choices

- **Primary pairing: Baratza Encore ESP, ZCG495, $199.95.** Baratza describes it as optimized for espresso and it makes the three separate-grinder setups comparable.
- **Comparison-only alternative: Fellow Opus 2, $199.95 for the standard black version checked.** It is explicitly intended for espresso and other brew methods, has 48 mm conical burrs and a stepless adjustment dial. Some finishes/configurations list at $249.95. It is a useful option for readers who switch brew methods, but it is not needed in each of the four featured pairs.

## Alternatives considered

- **Breville Barista Express, BES870XL, $699.95:** semi-automatic espresso machine with an integrated conical burr grinder (16 grind settings) and manual milk texturing. This is a strong alternative for someone who wants one machine with a grinder but still wants a hands-on espresso routine. Keep it as a comparison-page alternative for the machine-with-built-in-grinder question; the first-release four recommendations already cover the two separate-grinder price tiers, a traditional machine, and a one-touch automatic.
- **De’Longhi Magnifica Start ECAM22080SB EX:1, $799.95:** silver LatteCrema version of the selected machine. It reaches the top of the $800 band before tax, so the black ECAM22080B EX:1 at $749.95 is the more practical featured variant.
- **De’Longhi Magnifica Start ECAM22022B, $699.99:** the manufacturer lists this as a manual-milk version. It does not fit the featured one-touch-milk role.

These are selection decisions, not claims that excluded models are poor products. The shortlist is research-based and makes no hands-on testing claim.
