Before you pick

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need a separate grinder for espresso?

For a semi-automatic machine, usually yes. Espresso needs fine, repeatable adjustments; many general-purpose grinders don’t offer enough control. Fully automatic machines include a grinder.

Can I make milk drinks on the Bambino?

Yes. The standard Bambino has a manual steam wand. The Bambino Plus adds automatic milk texturing, while still letting you use a hands-on espresso workflow.
